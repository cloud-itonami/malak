malak.surveillance ethics-first surveillance scene + person search

Open-vocabulary scene search on street CCTV footage with warrant-gated person re-identification.
Designed for Japanese police investigation support and INTERPOL-member cross-border cooperation; ethics-by-design first, capability second.

設計の前提Design invariants

  1. 顔特徴量は国内 GPU 拠点内のみ。AES-256-GCM 暗号化で保管され、Cloudflare / 外部 LLM / 海外推論基盤への送信は protocol レベルで遮断されています。
  2. シーン記述検索と人物再特定を経路レベルで分離。属性検索は令状不要、既知人物の再特定は令状又は捜査関係事項照会書 + 上席承認がハードゲート。
  3. top-1 自動採用は不可能。人間判断介在義務 (human review gate) を Conditional edge で必須化、レビュー記録なしには証拠パケット出力に進めません。
  4. 音声トラックは ingest 時点で破棄。通信の秘密 (電気通信事業法4条) 配慮。
  5. 監査ログは 7 年保管。append-only、sha256 chain で改ざん検証可能。
  6. cooperation_status による国際 LEA フィルタ。INTERPOL 196 加盟国のうち sanctioned / restricted 国はoutreach 対象から hard-exclude。
  1. Face templates stay inside a JP on-prem GPU pod. AES-256-GCM at rest; egress to Cloudflare or non-JP inference providers is protocol-level blocked.
  2. Scene search and person re-identification are separated at the path level. Attribute-only search needs no warrant; known-person re-identification is hard-gated on warrant or formal-enquiry reference + supervisor approval.
  3. Top-1 auto-adoption is architecturally impossible. The human_review_gate is a Conditional edge in the LangGraph chain; no recorded review = no evidence packet.
  4. Audio is dropped at ingest. Compliant with Telecommunications Business Act §4 (secrecy of communication).
  5. Audit logs retained 7 years. Append-only, SHA-256 chain for tamper verification.
  6. International LEA filter by cooperation_status. Of 196 INTERPOL members, jurisdictions under sanctions / restricted cooperation are hard-excluded from outreach.

想定利用シーンUse cases

特殊詐欺の受け子同一性照合
複数現場の街頭カメラから類似属性の人物クリップを抽出。再特定は令状ゲート経由で確定。
行方不明者捜索
家族同意 + 警察依頼に基づき、人物特定情報を入力した再特定検索を実行。
街頭犯罪のシーン検索
「黒いフード・自転車・午後10時前後」のような自然言語記述で時間帯横断のクリップを抽出。
INTERPOL Red Notice 対象の国境横断追跡支援
INTERPOL Constitution Article 32 + 加盟国 NCB の協定に沿った国際協力。
Fraud-network mule re-identification
Extract candidate clips of attribute-matched persons across multiple incident sites; re-identification proceeds only via the warrant gate.
Missing-person investigation support
With family consent + LE request, run identification-input re-id search.
Open-vocabulary scene search for street crime
Extract clips by descriptions like "black hoodie, bicycle, around 22:00" — time/location-agnostic.
INTERPOL Red Notice cross-border tracking support
International cooperation aligned with INTERPOL Constitution Art. 32 and member-state NCB agreements.
